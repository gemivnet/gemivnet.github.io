No building update this time, but a couple of administrative notes.

## Replacement part

We decided to just replace the left lower spar cap for the horizontal
stabilizer. For $25.29, it was an easy decision.

## Kit shipping timeline

Vans emailed that both the wing and fuselage kits are expected to ship in
May. They asked for the final deposit, so I paid that this morning. It
officially looks like we have until May to finish the empennage kit, which
should be no problem at all. After that we'll have plenty to keep us busy
once we get back from our vacation in early May.

## All kits purchased

We also purchased the powerplant and finishing kits, so now every kit is
bought. We wanted to make sure there were no unexpected price increases,
supply chain issues, or anything else that might delay the build, so we
bought everything as soon as possible.

## Interior customization

We reached out to Abby at Flightline Interiors and we're going to do
significant customization to the interior. We'll head up to Wisconsin
closer to the finishing kit shipping time to pick out materials and
designs.
