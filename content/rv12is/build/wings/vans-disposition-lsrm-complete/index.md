## Vans disposition

Vans emailed me back about the two issues I posed (the incorrect rivet
countersinking and the scratch on the spar). Gary in Builder Support said
that while the rivet is slightly compromised, it is not advisable to remove
or modify anything there. He advised lightly sanding any rough or raised
edges and priming as required. (Not advice for your airplane, seek advice
from Vans!) So we're completely unblocked there.

## LSRM complete

Both Shelby and I are done with our LSRM class at Joby Aviation and we both
passed. We're waiting on the FSDO but we'll both have LSRM-A, and we both
now have our Rotax 9 Series iRMT Maintenance ratings!

The 6 day wrench week was awesome. We met some great people in our 11
person class and learned a lot. Thanks to Ben and Mike at Joby and Mike and
Lauren at MotiveAero for setting up an awesome program.

## Flightline Interiors

A couple of weekends ago we took a drive up to Wisconsin to meet with Abby
and her husband at Flightline Interiors. We discussed the interior design,
fabric options, and everything else. It was great to meet them in person
and we're really excited to see their work when it's done.

## Rib deburring

As for the plane, full steam ahead with deburring. Joby is an authorized
builder and we saw an S-LSA RV-12iS they were building. We asked about
their deburring methods for the ribs and they didn't have anything beyond
what we're already doing. They said it was 50-60 hours for rib deburring,
so we have a while to go.

We did find some new files while we were at the LSRM class. I bought a few
varieties and they're helping a bit, but it's still a slow process.

We're going to try to finish all of the ribs before we spend an entire day
cleaning, etching, and priming everything we have so far. That's going to
be a full day of work.

Don't expect any more pictures or updates until the rib deburring is
completed.

## Hours

Another 11 hours combined since the last update (2.1 for me, 7.9 for
Shelby, 1 for our exchange student). Wings are now at 40.2 combined hours.
