This is a collection of some things we did in Budapest. It's not an itinerary, just things we thought were cool and suggest trying if you have time.

## Restaurants

[VakVarjú](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KxkBSnvf87PZTpz36).

[Gettó Gulyás](https://maps.app.goo.gl/LMV2iJbqbsL6wfR6A).

[Il Terzo Cerchio](https://maps.app.goo.gl/hLLfiryRdvxtwtxU7). Our favorite restaurant in Budapest. Pizza.

[Panoráma Ételbár](https://maps.app.goo.gl/hd49bKcMLzRdC8A66). Food stall with the classics. Really good for a quick bite.

[Trofea Grill Restaurant](https://maps.app.goo.gl/86gBBCuGEz5znXvG8). Popular buffet.

[Melissa Bisztro Greek Taverna](https://maps.app.goo.gl/BG1Br7maAz8sJW2p8). The best gyros. Go late at night after drinking.

[Lángos Land](https://maps.app.goo.gl/y5ALqgQw4NczorMW6). A bit far from downtown but really good and in a really cool market. Make sure to get traditional, sour cream and cheese.

## Places

[Gellért-hegy](https://maps.app.goo.gl/PgQiSkdgmNwasi1u5). Fun hill to walk up with great views of the city.

[Gül Baba's Tomb & Rose Garden](https://maps.app.goo.gl/r27dCfACS1MYbWu29). Quiet, neat place to walk around.

[New York Café](https://maps.app.goo.gl/fh3f8ajfEPACvj2p8). Posh cafe.

[Museum of Ethnography](https://maps.app.goo.gl/L3jXrRjFG4bAYGC97). Not for the museum itself but there are some cool stairs you can walk up on top of the building.

[Gatehouse Tower](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kTdNNy4Ptr5yF6hBA). Cool castle in the park.

[Ronald Reagan Statue](https://maps.app.goo.gl/nc6b58SbpNZZuyyP8).

Király utca is a great street for drinking.

## Things to eat

You'll see these everywhere. Try all of them.

- Gulyásleves (goulash soup)
- Lángos
- Csirkepaprikás (chicken paprikash)
- Töltött káposzta (stuffed cabbage)
- Meggyleves (sour cherry soup)
- Pálinka
- Uborkasaláta (cucumber salad)
- Sült csülök (roasted pork knuckle)
- Kürtőskalács (chimney cake, you can't miss these, they're everywhere)
- Túró Rudi (look in convenience stores)
